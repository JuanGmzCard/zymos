💼 Commercial & Finance

Manage your brewery's entire financial operation: supplier invoices with line items and per-line VAT, beer sales to customers, purchase orders, and accounts receivable tracking.

Billing Inventory Administrator

Overview

The Commercial module groups five sub-areas that cover the complete money flow in the brewery:

🏭 Suppliers 🧾 Invoices 📋 Purchase orders 👥 Customers 💰 Sales

Supplier invoices are connected to inventory: when you register an invoice with ingredients, the stock is updated automatically. Sales are connected to production batches: each sale references the batch and packaging format.

Suppliers

Register your brewery's suppliers (maltsters, hop merchants, chemical distributors, etc.) so you can reference them in invoices and purchase orders.

  1. Go to Commercial → Suppliers → "+ New supplier".
  2. Fill in name, tax ID, email, phone, address, and notes.
  3. Save. The supplier becomes available in the invoice and PO forms.

To deactivate a supplier (hide it from forms without deleting it), edit it and disable the Active field. Historical records that reference it remain intact.

Supplier invoices

Register the invoices you receive from your ingredient suppliers. Each invoice can have multiple items (lines), each with its own category, quantity, unit price, and individual VAT.

Invoice items

Add one row for each line of the invoice. Click "+ Add item" for each product or service billed.

FieldDescription
TypeIngredient (for raw materials) or Equipment (for machinery and tools).
CategoryFor ingredients: Malt, Hop, Yeast, Clarifier, Chemical, Packaging. For equipment: Fermenter, Kettle, Chiller, etc.
NameItem name. As you type, the system suggests inventory ingredients based on the selected category.
QuantityQuantity purchased in the corresponding ingredient's unit.
Unit pricePrice per unit. If the "VAT included" field is active, the system extracts the price without VAT automatically.
VAT %VAT percentage for this specific item (e.g. 19%). Can differ on each line.
ℹ️
Automatic inventory update When you save the invoice, ZYMOS automatically adds the quantity of each item to the corresponding ingredient's stock (if the item name matches an inventory ingredient). When you edit the invoice, the system reverses the previous movement and applies the new one. When you delete the invoice, the stock is restored.

VAT handling

ZYMOS supports two VAT situations in supplier invoices:

Prices without VAT (default behavior)

The "VAT included" field is disabled. The unit prices you enter are the base prices without VAT. The system calculates the VAT by adding price × (VAT% / 100) for each line. Invoice total = subtotal + total VAT.

Prices with VAT included

Enable the "VAT included" field when your supplier's prices already include taxes (consumer-facing price). ZYMOS automatically extracts the price without VAT: price_without_vat = price_with_vat / (1 + VAT%/100). This option is common in retail invoices.

Invoice attachments

You can attach the PDF or image of each invoice directly in ZYMOS to maintain a digital backup of your accounting documents.

  1. Open the invoice detail.
  2. In the "Attachments" section, click "Upload file".
  3. Select the PDF or image (JPG, PNG) from your computer. Maximum size: 10 MB.
  4. The file is linked to the invoice and you can download or delete it at any time.

AI Invoice Analyzer 🤖

The invoice analyzer uses artificial intelligence (Claude Vision) to read the PDF or image of your invoice and automatically pre-fill the form fields: supplier, invoice number, date, and items with their quantities and prices.

⚠️
Requires AI configuration. The "Analyze document" button only appears when the administrator has configured the Anthropic API key (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY).
  1. Go to Commercial → Invoices → "+ New invoice".
  2. Click the "Analyze document" button at the top of the form.
  3. Select the PDF or image of the invoice from your computer (supported formats: PDF, JPG, PNG). Recommended maximum size: 10 MB.
  4. The AI processes the document and pre-fills the detected fields: invoice number, supplier, date, and items with quantities and prices.
  5. Review and correct the fields if necessary. The AI can make errors with low-quality documents or unusual formats.
  6. Save the invoice normally.
💡
The analyzer works best with digital PDFs (not scanned). For scanned images, make sure the resolution is at least 150 DPI for best results.

Purchase orders

Create purchase orders to request ingredients from your suppliers and keep a record of pending orders.

  1. Go to Commercial → Purchase orders → "+ New PO".
  2. Select the supplier, issue date, and expected delivery date.
  3. Add the requested items (name, quantity, estimated price).
  4. Save the PO. You can print it as a PDF to send to the supplier.

Purchase orders have statuses: Pending, In progress, Completed, and Cancelled. When the goods arrive, close the PO by changing its status to Completed and manually adjust the inventory stock.

⚠️
Purchase orders do not update the inventory automatically. You must manually adjust the ingredient stock when goods arrive (by editing the ingredient in Inventory).

Sales

Record each beer sale. A sale can include multiple items from different batches and packaging formats.

  1. Go to Commercial → Sales → "+ New sale".
  2. Select the customer (or type the name if not registered).
  3. Set the sale date and delivery date.
  4. Add items: select the batch, packaging format (330ml bottle, 20L keg, etc.), quantity, and price.
  5. Save. The sale is created with status Pending.

Sale statuses

Pending In progress Completed Overdue Cancelled

Update the sale status as it progresses: Pending when created, In progress when being prepared, Completed when delivered and paid.

💡
Packaging formats When selecting a batch, ZYMOS shows the packaging formats configured for that batch (e.g. "48 × 330ml Bottle | 2 × 20L Keg"). If the batch has no packaging destination configured, you can choose any format from the standard list.

Quotes

Generate quotes for customers who need a price proposal before confirming a purchase. Quotes have a configurable validity period (15 days by default).

  1. Go to Commercial → Sales → "New quote".
  2. Fill in the same fields as a sale (customer, items, prices).
  3. Set the quote expiration date (or leave the default value).
  4. Download the quote PDF to send to the customer.
  5. If the customer accepts, convert the quote to a sale with one click.

Export to PDF and Excel

All commercial modules have export options available at the top of the list:

  • PDF: generates a print-formatted document for individual invoices, POs, and listings.
  • Excel: exports the full list with all applied filters. Useful for taking data to your accountant or external accounting system.

Invoice statuses that trigger alerts

The dashboard shows an alert when there are invoices with status Received or Verified that have gone more than 30 days without being processed. This helps prevent invoices from remaining unpaid.
