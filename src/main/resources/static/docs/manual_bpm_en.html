BPM — Good Manufacturing Practices

The BPM module lets you document and audit the food safety practices of your brewery: staff health status, disinfectant solutions, pest control, waste disposal, and cleaning. All records generate a digitally signed PDF for regulatory inspections.

All staff Admin — Health authorizations

📋 Introduction

The BPM module covers the six food safety controls required by health regulations for craft breweries (INVIMA in Colombia and equivalents in other countries). Every record is digitally signed and can be exported as an official PDF for audits or inspections.

🩺

Health status

Mandatory daily check-in for each employee before entering the production area.

🧪

Disinfectant solutions

Documentation of each preparation: product, concentration, and responsible person.

🐛

Pest control

Periodic inspection of facility conditions and logging of pest sightings.

🗑️

Waste disposal

Record of waste removal with type, time, and responsible person.

🧹

Cleaning & sanitation

Cleaning control per area or utensil with approval sign-off.

ℹ️
How to access the module? The BPM module is in the side navigation bar, between Commercial and Admin. The base route is /bpm.

🔄 Daily mandatory flow

Each day, before using any part of ZYMOS, the system checks whether the user has completed their health check-in. This flow applies to all users except administrators.

User logs in → Health check-in today? → No → Redirects to /bpm/salud/diario
Symptoms declared? → Yes → Access suspended screen → Admin authorizes → Access restored
No symptoms? → Normal system access
⚠️
Administrators skip this flow. Users with the Administrator role can access the system without completing the daily health check-in. However, they may do so voluntarily from /bpm/salud/diario.

🩺 Daily health check-in

The health check-in is the first BPM control of the day. The system requests it automatically when a user enters ZYMOS without having completed it yet. It can also be submitted manually from /bpm/salud/diario.

How to complete the check-in

  1. Open the form The system redirects you automatically, or go to BPM › Health Status › Today's record.
  2. Review the symptoms Check all symptoms you are experiencing right now:
    Diarrhea Vomiting Fever Respiratory infection Skin lesion
    If you have none, leave all unchecked.
  3. Add observations (optional) You may write an additional note in the Observations field.
  4. Sign Draw your signature in the gray box using a mouse or your finger on a touch screen. If you make a mistake, use the Clear button and sign again.
  5. Save Click Register and continue. If you declared symptoms, you will be redirected to the suspended access screen. Otherwise, you will continue normally.
🚨
Only one check-in per day is allowed. If you already completed the check-in today, the form will show a summary of your current status and will not let you create a new one. If you made an error, contact your administrator.

If you already checked in today

When you open /bpm/salud/diario you will see your status for the day:

  • If you declared symptoms and are blocked: a "View access status" button.
  • If you have no symptoms or are authorized: a "Go to Dashboard" button.
  • In both cases: a "Download my record as PDF" button.

🔒 Access suspended due to symptoms

When an employee declares one or more symptoms, their system access is suspended until an administrator authorizes them. This ensures that no food handler with symptoms works without supervision.

🚫
What to do if your access is suspended? The screen will show the symptoms you declared. Contact your direct supervisor or administrator before proceeding. The only available button is "Log out".
💡
For administrators: once you have authorized the employee, tell them to reload the page — the system will re-check their status and grant access if the authorization was saved correctly.
📱
Automatic WhatsApp alert When an employee reports symptoms, ZYMOS generates an in-app notification and, if there are WhatsApp contacts subscribed to the "BPM Health" type under Admin → WhatsApp, it also sends a WhatsApp message so the administrator can act immediately.

✅ Health authorizations Admin only

This screen (/bpm/salud/autorizaciones) shows the employees who declared symptoms today and have not yet been authorized. Only administrators can access it.

How to authorize an employee

  1. Go to BPM › Authorizations The table shows the employee's name, declared symptoms, check-in time, and current status (Blocked / Authorized).
  2. Click "Sign and authorize" A modal appears with a digital signature box.
  3. Sign as responsible The signature is mandatory — the confirmation button will be inactive until you draw your signature. By signing, you are recorded as the person responsible for the authorization.
  4. Confirm Click Confirm authorization. The employee's status changes to Authorized and they will be able to access the system by reloading their page.
⚠️
By authorizing an employee with symptoms, you as the administrator assume responsibility for that decision. The signature is permanently stored in the record.

🧪 Disinfectant solutions

Document each disinfectant solution preparation: what product was used, in what amount, with how much water, and what final concentration was achieved.

📋 Create a new record
  1. Go to BPM › Disinfectant Solutions and click New record.
  2. Fill in the fields (see field table below).
  3. Select the product The Product field has autocomplete with chemicals registered in the Inventory. If the product does not appear, first add it under Inventory › New item (type: Chemical).
  4. Sign in the digital signature box.
  5. Save. The record will appear in the list.

Form fields

FieldDescriptionRequired
fechaDate the solution was preparedYes
horaTime of preparationNo
productoDisinfectant name (with suggestions from the Chemical inventory)Yes
cantidadAguaAmount of water used + unit (mL or L)Yes
cantidadProductoAmount of product + unit (mL, L, g, kg)Yes
concentracionFinalResulting concentration as a percentage (%)Yes
responsableUser who prepared the solution (list of tenant users)Yes
firmaDigital signature of the responsible personYes

✍️ Digital signature

All BPM forms include a digital signature box. Signatures are saved as images and appear in PDF reports.

How to use the signature box

  1. Draw with the mouse (desktop) or with your finger (mobile/tablet) inside the gray box.
  2. If you make a mistake, use the Clear button below the box to erase it and start over.
  3. The signature is saved automatically when you submit the form. No extra action is needed.
⚠️
The signature is mandatory on all BPM forms and health authorizations. The Save button will not work if the signature box is empty.

📄 Export to PDF

Each BPM sub-module allows exporting records to PDF. There are two export types:

TypeHow to accessContent
Period PDF Click the "PDF" button in the list header. You can filter by from and to dates (default: current month). All records in the period in a table sorted by date.
Individual PDF Click the "PDF" button in the specific record's row. A single record with all its fields, including the digital signature rendered as an image.
💡
To prepare documentation for a regulatory inspection, generate period PDFs with the date range requested by the inspector. The PDF automatically includes your brewery's name and logo.
