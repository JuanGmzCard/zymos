📦 Ingredient Inventory

Track the stock of all your production ingredients: malts, hops, yeasts, clarifiers, chemicals, and packaging. ZYMOS updates the inventory automatically every time you create or modify a batch.

Inventory Production Administrator

What is the inventory?

The Inventory module records all the ingredients your brewery uses to produce beer. Each ingredient has a type, a unit of measure, and an available quantity. When you create a production batch, ZYMOS automatically deducts the used ingredients.

Access the inventory from the main menu by clicking Inventory. The list shows all ingredients with their current stock, type, unit, and expiration date (if applicable).

💡
Prerequisite for creating batches Before registering your first production batch you need ingredients in the inventory. Without malts, hops, yeasts, or clarifiers registered, you won't be able to select them in the recipe or in the traceability form.

Ingredient types

ZYMOS organizes ingredients into 6 categories. The type determines which section the ingredient appears in when building recipes and batches.

Malt
Beer base: Pilsner, Munich, Crystal, Chocolate, etc.
Hop
Pellets or flowers: Citra, Cascade, Saaz, Amarillo, etc.
Yeast
Fermentation strains: American Ale, Saison, Lager, etc.
Clarifier
Irish Moss, Whirlfloc, Gelatin, Bentonite, etc.
Chemical
Products for cleaning, sanitation, and water treatment.
Packaging
Bottles, cans, caps, labels, capsules, etc.
✅
Tip — Chemical type for BPM Ingredients marked as Chemical appear in the BPM (Good Manufacturing Practices) module in the Sanitizing Solutions form. Register all your cleaning and sanitation products here so they can be referenced from BPM.

Automatic alerts

ZYMOS generates two types of alerts that appear in the Dashboard and in the inventory list:

🔴
Low stock The current quantity is below the minimum quantity configured for the ingredient.
⚠️
Expiring soon The expiration date is within the next 30 days.

Alerts also appear on the main Dashboard as quick-access cards: "Low stock" and "Expiring soon". Click on them to go directly to the filtered list.

📱
WhatsApp alerts If the administrator configured contacts in Admin → WhatsApp, these same alerts are also sent daily as WhatsApp messages to contacts subscribed to "Low stock" or "Expiration dates".

Add a new ingredient

  1. In the inventory list click the "+ New ingredient" button.
  2. Fill out the form with the ingredient details (see field table below).
  3. Click "Save". The ingredient appears immediately in the list.
Field Description Example
Name * Ingredient name as it will be recognized by producers when creating recipes. Weyermann Pilsner Malt
Type * Ingredient category (Malt, Hop, Yeast, Clarifier, Chemical, Packaging). Malt
Unit * Stock unit of measure. Options: gr, kg, mL, L, gal, und. kg
Quantity Current available stock. Can be left at zero when first registering the ingredient. 50
Minimum quantity Alert threshold. When the quantity drops below this value, the low stock alert appears. 5
Expiration date Optional. For hops, yeasts, and products with a limited shelf life. 06/15/2027
Price per unit Unit cost in the tenant's currency (e.g. $/kg). Used to estimate the production cost of a batch. 2.50
Notes Additional information: supplier, manufacturer lot, special characteristics. Lot #2024-A, supplier Fermentis
⚠️
Choose the unit carefully from the start The unit cannot be changed easily after the ingredient has been used in batches and recipes. If your malt arrives in 25 kg bags, register it in kg so recipe calculations are straightforward.

Edit and adjust stock

You can edit any ingredient data by clicking the edit button (pencil icon) in the corresponding row of the list.

Manual quantity adjustment

If you need to correct the stock (for example, after a physical inventory count), edit the ingredient and modify the Quantity field directly. There is no separate adjustment form — simply edit the ingredient and enter the correct quantity.

Common adjustment scenarios

  • Receiving goods: add the received quantity to the current stock.
  • Waste or loss: deduct the lost quantity.
  • Physical inventory: enter exactly the counted quantity.
  • Recording error: correct the incorrect value.

Filter and search

The inventory list has several filtering options at the top:

  • Text search: filters by ingredient name in real time.
  • Type filter: shows only Malt, only Hop, etc.
  • Low stock: filters only ingredients below their minimum.
  • Expiring soon: filters only ingredients expiring in the next 30 days.
💡
You can combine filters. For example: type=Hop + Low stock shows only the hops you need to restock.

Delete an ingredient

To delete an ingredient click the trash icon in its row. ZYMOS will ask for confirmation before proceeding.

🚫
You cannot delete ingredients that are in use If the ingredient is referenced in active recipes or batches, ZYMOS will prevent the deletion. You must first remove the ingredient from those records or complete/archive the corresponding batches.

Automatic deduction when producing

This is the most important integration between inventory and batch traceability:

What happens when you create a batch?

  1. When you save the batch with its ingredients, ZYMOS automatically deducts each ingredient from the stock.
  2. If the stock of any ingredient is insufficient to cover the batch quantity, you will receive a warning (but the batch is created anyway).
  3. If you edit the batch and change quantities, ZYMOS reverses the previous deduction and applies the new one.
  4. If you delete a batch, the stock of all its ingredients is restored.
ℹ️
Unit normalization ZYMOS automatically converts quantities to perform the deduction correctly. For example, if the ingredient is in kg and the batch specifies 5000 gr, ZYMOS deducts 5 kg from the stock. Supported conversions: kg ↔ gr, L ↔ mL, gal → mL.

Estimated production cost

If you add the Price per unit field to your ingredients, ZYMOS can automatically calculate the estimated production cost of a batch.

This calculation appears in the batch detail under the "Estimated cost" section, but only when the batch does not have purchase invoices assigned (real cost system). If you have invoices assigned, the real cost is shown instead of the estimate.

CalculationDescription
Total estimated cost Sum of (ingredient quantity × ingredient unit price) for all ingredients with a price.
Estimated cost per liter Total estimated cost ÷ final liters of the batch.

Relationship with purchase orders

The Purchase Orders module (in the Commercial section) lets you create orders to suppliers. However, purchase orders do not automatically update the inventory when created.

To record the receipt of goods into the inventory when an order arrives, you must manually adjust the quantity of the corresponding ingredient (edit the ingredient and add the received quantity).

💡
Recommended restocking workflow
  1. Detect the low stock alert in the Dashboard.
  2. Create a Purchase Order to the supplier from the Commercial module.
  3. When the goods arrive, edit the ingredient in Inventory and add the received quantity.
  4. Optionally, register the supplier Invoice in the Commercial module.
