Reports & Analytics

The Reports module consolidates production, sales and profitability data in one place. It offers historical charts, filterable tables and PDF/Excel export for business analysis.

Administrator · Manager · Production

📊 Introduction

The Reports module is available in the navigation bar. It contains three independent reports, each with their own filters, statistics and charts. All reports allow exporting the filtered data to PDF or Excel.

ℹ️
Access path: /reportes — automatically redirects to the Production Report. The others are accessed from the navigation tabs.

🔀 Navigating between reports

At the top of each report there are three tabs to switch between reports without losing your date filters:

📦 Production
💰 Sales
📈 Profitability

The active tab is highlighted. Click any tab to switch reports.

🔍 Filters & periods

All reports share the same date filtering logic. Filters are applied immediately on click.

FilterDescription
From / ToDate range. Defaults to the last 3 months.
This monthQuick shortcut: from the 1st of the current month to today.
Last monthQuick shortcut: complete previous month.
3 monthsQuick shortcut: last 90 days.
This yearQuick shortcut: from January 1st of the current year.
StyleProduction and Profitability only. Filter by beer style.
StatusSales only. Filter by status: Shipped, Pending, Cancelled.
💡
Filters also apply to PDF and Excel exports. What you see on screen is exactly what gets downloaded.

📈 Profitability Report

Calculates the gross margin of each batch by comparing revenue from shipped sales against assigned production costs.

⚠️
For the margin to appear, the batch must have both: assigned costs (from the batch form, Costs tab) AND shipped sales. A batch without costs or without sales will appear with a special label instead of the margin.

Margin statuses per batch

LabelMeaning
ProfitableRevenue exceeds costs — positive margin.
At a lossCosts exceed revenue — negative margin.
No salesBatch has costs but no shipped sales yet.
No costBatch has sales but no assigned production costs.

Profitability table

Columns: Batch Code, Style, Liters, Total Cost, Revenue, Gross Margin, Margin %, Status. The totals row at the bottom sums all batches in the period.

You can sort the table by clicking the column headers.

Export: 📄 PDF 📊 Excel

The Profitability Excel contains 3 sheets: Profitability (by batch), By Style (summary), Monthly Trend (margin evolution).

🤖 AI Profitability Analysis

The AI profitability analysis processes the current report data and generates an automatic diagnosis: it detects the most and least profitable style, identifies trends and generates actionable recommendations to improve business margins.

ℹ️
This feature requires AI configuration (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY). If the "Analyze with AI" button does not appear in the report, the system is running without AI.

How to use the AI analysis

StepAction
1In the Profitability Report, apply the date and style filters you want to analyze.
2Click "Analyze with AI" at the top of the report.
3The modal shows: most profitable style, least profitable style, period trend, key insights and specific recommendations with attention alerts.
💡
The analysis is based on the current filtered report data. For a different analysis, close the modal, change the filters and open it again.
