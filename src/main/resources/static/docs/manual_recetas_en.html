📖 Recipes

Create and manage your beer formulas: malts, hops, yeasts, clarifiers, mash steps, and boil additions. Scale any recipe to a different volume with a single click.

Production Administrator

What is a recipe?

A recipe in ZYMOS is the complete formula for producing a specific beer. It includes the ingredient list with quantities, the mash profile (temperature and duration), and the schedule of hop and clarifier additions during the boil.

Recipes are the foundation of the Traceability module: when you create a production batch, you select a recipe and ZYMOS pre-loads all its ingredients automatically.

⚠️
Prerequisite for production ZYMOS requires at least one active recipe to create batches. If you have no recipes yet, the Traceability module will automatically redirect you to create one.

Recipe list

Access from the main menu → Recipes. The list shows all active recipes (archived ones are hidden by default). For each recipe you can see:

  • Name and beer style
  • Target volume in liters
  • Target OG / FG (gravities)
  • Estimated ABV
  • Actions: View, Edit, Duplicate, Archive

Ingredients

The form divides ingredients into four sections. You can add as many rows as needed in each one by clicking "+ Add".

🌿 Hops

🦠 Yeasts
Name · Quantity · Unit (gr/und)
💧 Clarifiers
Name · Quantity · Unit (gr/mL/und)

The Name field for each ingredient has autocomplete: as you type, ZYMOS suggests inventory items that match the type of that section (malts for malts, hops for hops, etc.).

The ingredient doesn't appear in the list?

If the ingredient you're looking for doesn't exist in the inventory, a ⊞ button appears at the end of the name field. Clicking it opens a quick-create modal that lets you register the ingredient without leaving the recipe form.

The ingredient created from the modal is registered in the inventory with quantity 0. Remember to update its stock when you receive it.

Save the recipe

  1. Fill in all required fields (marked with *): name, style, and volume.
  2. Add at least one ingredient in any of the sections (malt, hop, yeast, or clarifier).
  3. Click "Save recipe" at the bottom of the form.
  4. ZYMOS validates the data and redirects to the detail page of the newly created recipe.

View recipe detail

From the list, click on the recipe name or the eye button to view its full detail. The detail page shows:

  • Target parameters: volume, OG, FG, estimated ABV, efficiency, mash and sparge water
  • Ingredient table by type
  • Mash steps
  • Boil addition schedule
  • Interactive scale calculator
  • Action buttons: Edit, Duplicate, Create batch, Archive

  1. In the recipe detail, find the "Scale calculator" section.
  2. Enter the target volume in liters in the corresponding field.
  3. The ingredient table updates in real time showing the scaled quantities.
  4. If you want to save the scaled version as a new recipe, click "Create scaled recipe". ZYMOS creates a copy with the adjusted quantities and an updated name.
💡
The scaled recipe is independent of the original. You can edit it, adjust ingredients, and use it to create batches without affecting the base recipe.

Duplicate a recipe

Duplicating creates an exact copy of the recipe with the name Copy of [original name]. It is useful for creating variations (for example, a dry-hop version with more hops) without altering the base recipe.

  1. In the recipe list, click the duplicate icon in the recipe row.
  2. ZYMOS creates the copy and redirects you to its edit form.
  3. Change the name and adjust whatever you need.
  4. Save the modified recipe.

Archive a recipe

Archiving hides the recipe from the active list and from batch creation forms. It is not deleted — you can recover it in the future.

ℹ️
Difference between archiving and deleting ZYMOS does not delete recipes that have been used in batches — instead it archives them. This preserves production history: existing batches continue to show the data of the recipe that originated them.

To see archived recipes, enable the "Show archived" filter in the list. From there you can restore them if you need them again.

Use a recipe to create a batch

There are two ways to start a production batch from a recipe:

Option A — From the recipe detail

  1. Open the recipe detail.
  2. Click "Create batch".
  3. ZYMOS opens the new batch form with the recipe pre-selected.

Option B — From Traceability

  1. Go to Traceability → "New batch".
  2. In the form, select the recipe in the "Recipe" field.
  3. Click "Load recipe" to pre-fill the ingredients automatically.
💡
Loading the recipe in the batch form is a starting point — you can freely adjust quantities before saving the batch. Changes to the batch do not affect the original recipe.

Water profile

The water profile describes the mineral composition of the water you will use for your recipe. These parameters influence mash pH, flavor, and bitterness perception.

All fields are optional. They are shown in the recipe detail and included in the BeerXML export.

MineralUnitTypical rangeMain effect
Calcium (Ca²⁺)ppm50–150 ppmClarity, enzyme stability, lowers pH
Magnesium (Mg²⁺)ppm10–30 ppmSalty/bitter flavor at high concentrations
Sodium (Na⁺)ppm0–150 ppmRounds the flavor, intensifies sweetness at low amounts
Sulfate (SO₄²⁻)ppm50–300 ppmAccentuates dry bitterness in hoppy beers
Chloride (Cl⁻)ppm50–200 ppmRounds body, intensifies malt character
Bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻)ppm0–300 ppmAlkalinity, raises mash pH
Water notes——Notes about the water source or adjustments made
💡
A high sulfate/chloride ratio (>1.5) favors hoppy, dry beers. A low ratio (<0.5) favors malty, round beers.

Export / Import BeerXML

BeerXML 1.0 is the industry standard format for exchanging recipes between brewing software (Beersmith, Brewer's Friend, etc.). ZYMOS can export and import recipes in this format.

Export a recipe

  1. Open the detail of the recipe you want to export.
  2. Click the "BeerXML" button in the detail header.
  3. A .xml file downloads with all the recipe data: ingredients, mash steps, boil additions, water profile, and target gravities.

Import a recipe from BeerXML

  1. In the recipe list, click "Import BeerXML".
  2. In the modal that appears, select the .xml file from your computer.
  3. ZYMOS processes the file, creates the recipe, and redirects you to its edit form so you can adjust the data before saving.
ℹ️
The imported BeerXML can include malts, hops, yeasts, clarifiers, mash steps, and water profile. Ingredients are created in the inventory with quantity 0 if they don't already exist.

AI Recipe Assistant 🤖

The AI recipe assistant generates a complete recipe from a free-text description. Describe the type of beer you want and the AI proposes ingredients, gravities, and production parameters.

⚠️
Requires AI configuration. This feature is only available when the administrator has configured the Anthropic API key (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY). If the button doesn't appear, the system runs without AI.

How to use the assistant

  1. In the recipe list, click "Create recipe with AI".
  2. In the modal, describe the beer you want to produce: style, flavor notes, body, bitterness, volume. For example: "A 20-liter tropical IPA with mango and passion fruit notes, moderate bitterness, 75% efficiency".
  3. Click "Generate recipe". The AI takes a few seconds to process.
  4. Review the proposal in the modal preview panel.
  5. If the proposal looks good, click "Use this recipe". ZYMOS pre-fills the new recipe form with all the generated data.
  6. Adjust whatever you need and save normally.
💡
The AI takes into account the ingredients available in your inventory to suggest items you already have registered. You can be as specific or as general as you like in the description.
